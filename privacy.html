Privacy Policy

Bemny Studio · Last updated 13 October 2026

Bemny Studio is a private tool that Bemny Technologies uses to make videos and upload them to its own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what happens when a Google account is connected to it.

What Bemny Studio asks for

When a YouTube channel is connected, Bemny Studio asks Google for one permission: to upload videos to that channel (the youtube.upload permission of the YouTube Data API). It uses that permission only to upload a video that a person has approved, with its title, description and tags, and to set that video's thumbnail.

What it stores

  • An access token from Google. After you give permission, Google issues a token that lets Bemny Studio upload to your channel. It is kept in a file on the computer Bemny Studio runs on, and nowhere else.
  • A record of what it uploaded. For each video it uploads, it keeps the video's YouTube address and the time of the upload, on the same computer.

It does not store your Google password, which it never sees, and it does not collect your name, email address, contacts, viewing history, comments or channel statistics.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, change or delete existing videos, playlists, comments or account settings.
  • It does not share, sell or transfer anything from your Google account to anyone.
  • It does not use anything from your Google account for advertising, or to train artificial intelligence models.
  • It has no analytics, tracking or cookies. This website has none either.

Google's rules that apply

Bemny Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Bemny Studio uploads through YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How to withdraw access and have data deleted

  • You can withdraw Bemny Studio's access at any time on Google's security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Its token stops working at once.
  • To have the stored token and upload records deleted from our computer, write to the address below. They are deleted within seven days.

Videos already uploaded stay on the YouTube channel until its owner removes them in YouTube.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
