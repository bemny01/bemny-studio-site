Data Deletion

Bemny Studio · Last updated 20 October 2026

Bemny Studio keeps only what it needs to post to accounts that have been connected to it: their access tokens, the IDs and names of the Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, and a record of the addresses and times of what it posted. All of it is kept on the one computer Bemny Studio runs on. The privacy policy describes it in full.

1. Remove Bemny Studio's access

This stops it working at once, before anything is deleted.

  • Facebook and Instagram: in Facebook, open Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Business integrations (on some accounts, Apps and websites). Find Bemny Studio and choose Remove.
  • Google and YouTube: open myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Bemny Studio and remove its access.

2. Ask for the stored data to be deleted

  1. Write to dubemantony@gmail.com with the subject "Bemny Studio data deletion".
  2. Name the Facebook Page, Instagram account or YouTube channel concerned.
  3. Within seven days its tokens, IDs and post records are deleted from Bemny Studio's computer, and you get a reply saying so.

What deletion does not cover

Videos and posts already published stay on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram until the account's owner removes them there. Deleting Bemny Studio's records does not take them down.
