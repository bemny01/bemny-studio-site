Bemny Studio

A private production tool made and used by Bemny Technologies.

Bemny Studio is the software Bemny Technologies uses to make its own educational videos: it helps research and write a script, prepare narration and pictures, and put the finished video together. When a video has been reviewed and approved by a person, Bemny Studio can upload it to the YouTube channel it was made for.

Who uses it

Only Bemny Technologies. Bemny Studio is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not a service other people can use. It runs on our own computer.

Why it asks for access to a Google account

To upload a finished video, Bemny Studio asks the owner of a YouTube channel for permission to upload videos to that channel. That is the only permission it asks for. It uses it to:

  • upload a video, with its title, description and tags, to the channel that was chosen;
  • set that video's thumbnail picture.

It does not read, change or delete anything else in the account, and it never uploads anything until a person has approved the video and asked for it to be posted.

Privacy

What Bemny Studio stores and how to withdraw its access is set out in the privacy policy.
